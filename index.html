Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2025

Status Saver is developed and maintained by Grey Matrix (https://greymatrix.netlify.app). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information when you use Status Saver.

1. Information We Access

Status Saver does not collect or store any personal data on external servers. All operations are performed locally on your device.

The app accesses:

  • Photos and videos from WhatsApp Status folders
  • Media saved by the user using the app

This access is required solely to display and save WhatsApp status media as requested by the user.

2. Permissions

The app may request the following permissions:

  • Media / Storage access – to read and save photos and videos
  • Internet access – to display ads and verify subscriptions

We do not access files beyond what is required for the app’s core functionality.

3. Advertisements

Status Saver uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

AdMob may collect certain information such as device identifiers, advertising ID, and interaction data in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy.

Learn more: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. In-App Purchases

The app offers a subscription to remove ads.

All payments and subscription management are handled securely by Google Play Billing. Status Saver does not process or store any payment information.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third party.

6. Children’s Privacy

Status Saver does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
